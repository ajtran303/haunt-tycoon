# Design notes

Research and rationale that back the roadmap but don't drive work. The roadmap (ROADMAP.md) is the work order.

## The industry calendar being compressed

A real haunt's year, and where each beat lands in the game:

| Months | What really happens | In the game |
|---|---|---|
| March | TransWorld trade show: buy animatronics, masks, effects, often used from folded haunts | Deferred (multi-season shop; single-season folds it into build budget) |
| April-August | Design and construction; fire-code materials, permits | Phase 1: build |
| Late summer | Fire marshal inspection: occupancy, egress | End of phase 1: occupancy cap that caps line pace |
| August-September | Open casting calls, auditions | Phase 2: auditions |
| September | Actor training week ("scare school"), makeup and blocking rehearsals | Phase 3: scare school |
| September | Marketing push: billboards, radio, social; presale tickets on sale | Phase 4: marketing |
| Late September | Media preview night, friends-and-family soft open | Phase 5: press preview |
| Week before doors | Maximum leverage: everything spent, nothing earned | The squeeze (asserted in exit criteria) |
| October | The season; revenue concentrated on Fri/Sat and Halloween week | Milestones 1, 2, 4 |
| November | Blackout-weekend variants, then prop resale and warehouse storage | Deferred |
